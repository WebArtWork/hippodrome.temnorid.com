About the game

Breeding, training and racing horses.

Breed and train horses, feed them well and bring them to the track. A horse's speed, stamina and temper, together with your sense of pacing, decide who crosses the line first.

Start at village fairs, then compete in city tournaments with prizes and betting. Wins bring bloodlines, saddles and horse armour that help in Conquest and the Arena.

Graphics are simple placeholder shapes for now. The game is built to be played first and polished later.

How to play

You on a horse against computer riders, three laps around one track. In the main menu choose Play for a race on your own, Play with others for a shared race, or Profile for your name, colour and account.

  1. Press Start Race. A countdown runs, then the race begins.
  2. Accelerate, brake and steer your horse around the track. The camera follows behind. Keep clear of the other riders and the rails: both slow you down.
  3. Pass every checkpoint in order; a lap only counts if you did. Cutting across the field does not.
  4. Twice a lap the track narrows between hay bales, with a bar across the gap. Jump it with Space.
  5. Watch your lap, your time and your position among the riders.
  6. After the last lap you see the final order and your time. Restart to race again.

You win

Finish three valid laps ahead of the other riders.

You lose

There is no losing, only a place. Finish first to win; your best lap and best time are saved.

Riding

Computer riders

They race to win. They go round slower horses, use their own bursts of speed and stay on your tail when you lead, but they make mistakes too: one now and then mistimes a jump.

Other riders

Horses do not pass through each other. Close behind another horse yours eases off, and horses side by side slow down and are pushed apart, so steer round.

Walls

A rail or a hay bale takes speed away, and scraping along it keeps taking it.

Boost

Shift gives a burst of speed for about a second, then the horse needs a short rest. Press it too often and the horse tires, runs slower and stops answering until it has caught its breath. Watch the stamina bar.

Jump

Space makes the horse jump. Be in the air when the front of the horse reaches the bar, or it stumbles and loses most of its speed.

Keyboard on a PC, touch on a phone. Your best lap and best time are saved on your device and shown under Records.

Playing with others

Up to four people can ride the same race. Choose Play with others in the main menu, then either host a game here (friends on the same network type the address it shows) or host online (friends type a short code), or join with an address or a code. Everybody presses Ready and the host starts. Each person rides their own horse, the computer riders fill the empty places, and the catch-up help the computer riders give in a single-player race is switched off.

Phones

Android and iOS builds are made from Unity Build Profiles. On a phone there are buttons to steer left and right, Brake, Boost and Jump; the places (Race and Records) are along the bottom.

Updates

The Windows game updates itself from the newest GitHub release when it starts. You can turn this off in Profile. On Android and iOS the update comes from the store or the release page.

Part of the world of Temnorid

Horses from the Hippodrome will join the armies of Lords, and bloodlines, saddles and horse armour will help in Conquest and the Arena.

Today Temnorid Hippodrome does not talk to Lords. The first time you press Play or Play with others you can sign in with the same account as in Lords (Google, on Windows), play as a guest, or play offline; you can also sign in later from Profile. An account only says who you are: it is not needed to race, a single-player race works without an internet connection, and nothing is read from or written to Lords.
